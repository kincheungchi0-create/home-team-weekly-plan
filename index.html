Nutrition Planning Rules

  • Power / Howard calciumAim to include calcium-rich food every day: milk, plain yogurt, cheese, tofu, calcium-set bean curd, calcium-fortified soy milk, or dark green vegetables.
  • Power / Howard school lunchOn Monday to Friday, Howard/Power eats lunch at school, so home lunch tofu or vegetables should not be counted for his calcium or fiber intake.
  • After-school calciumOn school days, add an after-school calcium snack such as warm milk, plain yogurt, cheese bread, milk custard or banana milk.
  • Power / Howard daily balanceAcross breakfast, lunch and dinner, include grains, protein, vegetables/fruit and a calcium source. If dinner has more meat, keep portions smaller and add more vegetables.
  • Hazel meal structureHazel lunch and dinner should each include meat/fish plus two vegetables, mixed into congee / noodles / soft rice and chopped very small.
  • Hazel allergy / sensitivityDo not give Hazel egg or pumpkin. Avoid egg yolk, steamed egg, pancake with egg, pumpkin puree and pumpkin rice for Hazel.
  • Hazel protein rotationDo not repeat Hazel's lunch and dinner protein on the same day. Rotate fish, beef, pork and chicken through the week.
  • Shared ingredientsUse safe parts of the adult meal for Hazel where possible, such as fresh fish, tomato, chayote, spinach or broccoli, but cook separately without salt, soy sauce or strong seasoning.
  • Fiber checkDinner should always include a clear vegetable dish. Use different vegetables across the week to avoid relying only on choi sum or broccoli.
  • Protein checkAvoid stacking too many heavy protein dishes for the kids in one dinner. If there are two protein dishes, keep one light and increase vegetables.

Critical Safety Rules

  • Kitchen safetyDo not bring Hazel or Howard into the kitchen. Only if there is no other choice and no one can watch Hazel, she may stay in the high chair for a short time.
  • Hot dishesWhen taking out hot dishes, use two thick towels and hold both sides carefully. Do not use dish tongs to clamp hot plates or bowls.
  • High chair safetyHazel can climb. Never leave her alone in the high chair. Put her in the playpen or baby cot if you need to step away.
  • Small toysKeep Howard's small toys away from Hazel's playmat. Put small or unsafe toys into a drawer or bag.
  • Food safetyAlways check expiry dates and freshness before cooking, especially meat, fish and Hazel's food.
  • Hazel food allergyHazel should not eat egg or pumpkin. Please check ingredients before giving her pancakes, steamed egg, egg yolk, pumpkin puree or pumpkin rice.
  • Fever / vaccinationIf Hazel has fever or after vaccination, monitor her temperature. Tell Mom immediately if fever is over 38C.
