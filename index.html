Nutrition Planning Rules

  • Power / Howard calciumAim to include calcium-rich food every day: milk, plain yogurt, cheese, tofu, calcium-set bean curd, calcium-fortified soy milk, or dark green vegetables.
  • Power / Howard school lunchOn Monday to Friday, Howard/Power eats lunch at school, so home lunch tofu or vegetables should not be counted for his calcium or fiber intake.
  • After-school calciumOn school days, add an after-school calcium snack such as warm milk, plain yogurt, cheese bread, milk custard or banana milk.
  • Power / Howard daily balanceAcross breakfast, lunch and dinner, include grains, protein, vegetables/fruit and a calcium source. If dinner has more meat, keep portions smaller and add more vegetables.
  • Hazel meal structureHazel lunch and dinner should each include 1 protein plus 2 vegetables, mixed into congee / noodles / soft rice and chopped very small.
  • Hazel protein rotationDo not repeat Hazel's lunch and dinner protein on the same day. Rotate fish, beef, pork and chicken through the week.
  • Shared ingredientsUse safe parts of the adult meal for Hazel where possible, such as fresh fish, tomato, pumpkin, spinach or broccoli, but cook separately without salt, soy sauce or strong seasoning.
  • Fiber checkDinner should always include a clear vegetable dish. Use different vegetables across the week to avoid relying only on choi sum or broccoli.
  • Protein checkAvoid stacking too many heavy protein dishes for the kids in one dinner. If there are two protein dishes, keep one light and increase vegetables.

Critical Safety Rules

  • Kitchen safetyDo not bring Hazel or Howard into the kitchen. Only if there is no other choice and no one can watch Hazel, she may stay in the high chair for a short time.
  • Hot dishesWhen taking out hot dishes, use two thick towels and hold both sides carefully. Do not use dish tongs to clamp hot plates or bowls.
  • High chair safetyHazel can climb. Never leave her alone in the high chair. Put her in the playpen or baby cot if you need to step away.
  • Small toysKeep Howard's small toys away from Hazel's playmat. Put small or unsafe toys into a drawer or bag.
  • Food safetyAlways check expiry dates and freshness before cooking, especially meat, fish and Hazel's food.
  • Fever / vaccinationIf Hazel has fever or after vaccination, monitor her temperature. Tell Mom immediately if fever is over 38C.

Warm Reminders For Helpers

  • Kitchen safetyPlease do not bring Hazel or Howard into the kitchen while cooking or handling hot food. Only use the high chair in the kitchen if there is really no other choice.
  • Hot dishesPlease use two thick towels to take out hot dishes. Hold both sides carefully and do not use dish tongs to clamp hot plates or bowls.
  • Baby safetyPlease handle Hazel gently when helping her sleep. Do not shake her too much.
  • High chairHazel can climb now, so please do not leave her alone in the high chair. Use the playpen or baby cot if needed.
  • Milk temperaturePlease check milk temperature carefully before feeding Hazel. It is usually around 41-42C, warm but not too hot.
  • Food temperatureHazel's congee and soft food should also be warm only, around or below 40C.
  • Food texturePlease chop Hazel's meat and vegetables very small, and keep her congee less watery so she can learn to chew.
  • No seasoning for HazelPlease cook Hazel's food soft, fully cooked, with no strong seasoning, no added salt and no soy sauce.
  • Fish and shrimpFor Hazel, remove all fish bones carefully. Cut shrimp into very small cubes.
  • Fresh ingredientsPlease always check freshness and expiry dates before cooking, especially for Hazel and Howard.
  • Market shoppingWhen buying ingredients, please also check whether Hazel's food, fruit, milk items or baby ingredients need restocking.
  • Morning baby areaPlease wipe Hazel's playmat and high chair in the morning and set up the play area nicely.
  • Fruit and waterIf Hazel's poop is hard, please give more fruit and water during the day.
  • Sleep routineIf Hazel is sleeping during milk time and you are not sure what to do, please ask first. Usually her milk should still be given on time.
  • ClothingIf Hazel is wearing too many layers before sleep, please remove one layer so she does not get too hot.
  • Bath and clothesAfter Hazel goes out or plays downstairs, please help change her into clean clothes and follow the bath routine.
  • ToysPlease keep small toys away from Hazel's play area because she may put them into her mouth.
  • Howard routinePlease help Howard with school snacks, shower after school, and homework or rest time when needed.
  • Howard school clothesPlease prepare Howard's clothes the night before: school uniform on Monday, Tuesday and Friday; sportswear on Wednesday and Thursday.
  • Cups and bottlesPlease wash cup and water-bottle edges carefully every morning. Use dishwashing detergent for tea stains and rinse well.
  • Kitchen routinePlease keep the kitchen tidy after cooking, store leftovers properly, and check the next day's ingredients at night.
  • Housework planningPlease manage market time well and try to finish shopping efficiently, so home duties can stay on schedule.
